1. Mele Kalikimaka Mai Tai

Double Rum Legend
The King of Tiki with Festive Caribbean Soul

Formula: 30ml Dark Jamaican Rum, 30ml White Rum, 20ml Fresh Lime Juice, 15ml Orgeat Almond Syrup, 15ml Orange Curaçao. Shake with ice; strain over crushed ice; float dark rum.

Fabulous Garnish Fresh mint sprig pine-tree, maraschino cherry & tropical umbrella.

2. Blue Hawaiian Blizzard

Electric Blue Lagoon
Frosted Tropical Aqua Blue with Creamy Coconut Snow

Formula: 45ml Coconut Rum (or Vodka), 30ml Blue Curaçao Liqueur, 60ml Pineapple Juice, 30ml Rich Cream of Coconut. Shake vigorously with ice into a vivid frosty glow.

Fabulous Garnish Toasted coconut shredded snow rim, fresh pineapple slice & red cherry.

3. Santa's Tiki Sunrise

Layered Sun Ombre
Vibrant Island Sunset with Glowing Festive Crimson

Formula: 45ml White Island Rum, 90ml Passion-Orange-Guava (POG) Juice, 20ml Grenadine sinker. Build directly in glass over ice, slowly drizzling grenadine down the inside rim.

Fabulous Garnish Edible red hibiscus blossom, dehydrated blood orange wheel & bamboo straw.

4. Coconut Snow Drift Colada

Velvet Island Cream
Silky Tropical White Velvet with Festive Holiday Spice

Formula: 50ml Spiced Coconut Rum, 60ml Pineapple Puree, 45ml Coconut Cream, 15ml Lime Juice. Shake over crushed ice for a frothy, snowy texture.

Fabulous Garnish Cinnamon-sugar glazed rim, freshly grated nutmeg dusting & mint holly sprig.

5. Virgin Palm Spritz (Mocktail)

0.0% Alcohol
Crisp, Effervescent & Vibrant Tropical Cooler

Formula: 60ml Guava Nectar, 60ml Pineapple Juice, 20ml Fresh Lime, topped with Fiery Island Ginger Beer and a splash of Cranberry.

Fabulous Garnish Candied lime wheel, fresh cranberries, and a miniature paper tiki umbrella.

🍹 5-Minute Batching Tip

Pre-batch Mai Tai and Blue Hawaiian in 2-liter pitchers (without ice) ahead of party rush. Just shake 150ml of mix with fresh ice and garnish in 10 seconds!

🥥 Show-Stopping Garnish Secret

Dip rims in honey before rolling in toasted desiccated coconut or festive cinnamon sugar. Add mint slapped between hands to release fragrant holiday oils!
