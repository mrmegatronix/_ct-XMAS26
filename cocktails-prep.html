1. Mele Kalikimaka Mai Tai

Highball / Tiki Mug
  • 30ml White Rum
  • 20ml Fresh Lime Juice
  • 15ml Orgeat (Almond Syrup)
  • 15ml Orange Curaçao / Triple Sec
  • 30ml Dark Rum (Float on top)
  1. Add White Rum, Lime, Orgeat, and Curaçao to shaker with cubed ice.
  2. Shake hard for 8 seconds; strain into glass filled with crushed ice.
  3. Slowly pour 30ml Dark Rum over back of spoon to float on top.
🌺 GARNISH: Slap fresh mint sprig (pine-tree look), lime wheel, cherry & paper umbrella.

2. Blue Hawaiian Blizzard

Hurricane Glass
  • 45ml Coconut Rum (or Vodka)
  • 30ml Blue Curaçao
  • 60ml Pineapple Juice
  • 30ml Cream of Coconut
  1. Rim Hurricane glass with honey & roll in shredded coconut ("snow rim").
  2. Combine all 4 liquids in shaker with ice and shake vigorously until frothy.
  3. Pour unstrained into rimmed glass (top with extra crushed ice if needed).
🥥 GARNISH: Toasted/white coconut rim, fresh pineapple wedge & maraschino cherry.

3. Santa's Tiki Sunrise (No Shaker)

Tall Collins Glass
  • 45ml White or Gold Island Rum
  • 90ml POG Juice (Passion-Orange-Guava)
  • 20ml Grenadine Syrup (Sinker)
  1. Fill tall glass to the top with fresh cubed ice.
  2. Pour Rum and POG juice directly over ice and give a quick stir.
  3. Pour 20ml Grenadine slowly down inside edge of glass so it settles at bottom.
🌅 GARNISH: Dehydrated orange wheel, edible hibiscus flower & bamboo straw (DO NOT STIR).

4. Coconut Snow Drift Colada

Coconut Cup / Hurricane
  • 50ml Spiced Rum
  • 60ml Pineapple Juice
  • 45ml Coconut Cream
  • 15ml Fresh Lime Juice
  1. Rim glass with lime wedge and dip in cinnamon-sugar mix.
  2. Add Spiced Rum, Pineapple, Coconut Cream, and Lime to shaker with ice.
  3. Shake hard for 10 seconds for thick velvet foam; strain over fresh ice.
🎅 GARNISH: Dust foam with grated nutmeg & add 2 mint leaves + 1 red cherry ("holly").
